Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Cavitronix AI does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

  • The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
  • All calculations are performed on your device.
  • Data you enter or import (pressures, flows, test points, vibration files) is used only inside the app.
  • If you save a project, the project file is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud Drive account, under your Apple ID. The developer cannot access it.
  • Files you export (for example CSV) are saved or shared only where you choose.

Children

The app is an engineering tool, is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone.
